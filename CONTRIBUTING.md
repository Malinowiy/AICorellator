Thank you for considering a contribution. This document explains how
to contribute and the terms under which contributions are accepted.

## Before you contribute

1.0.0**, with a separate commercial license available. To keep this
model workable, we need contributions to be licensed in a way that
allows us to offer both the noncommercial and the commercial license.

This means: **by submitting a contribution, you agree to the
Contributor License Agreement below.**

If you are not comfortable with these terms, please do not submit
code, documentation, or other material. You are welcome to open issues
and provide feedback without a CLA.

## How to contribute

1. **Open an issue first** for anything non-trivial. This avoids
   wasted effort if the change is out of scope.

2. **Fork the repository** and create a branch for your change.

3. **Write tests.** All contributions must include tests covering the
   new behavior. The project uses pytest.

4. **Run the test suite** before submitting: `pytest tests/ -v`.

5. **Open a pull request** with a clear description of what changes
   and why.

6. **Include the CLA statement** in your pull request:

   > I have read the Contributor License Agreement in CONTRIBUTING.md
   > and agree to its terms.

---

## What we look for

- **Correctness.** The change does what it claims.
- **Tests.** New behavior is covered.
- **Consistency.** The change fits the existing architecture and style.
- **Scope.** The change is focused; unrelated refactors are separate
  pull requests.

## What we may decline

- Changes that break existing tests without a strong reason.
- Changes that expand scope beyond the project's purpose.
- Changes that introduce dependencies without discussion.
- Changes from contributors who have not agreed to the CLA.
