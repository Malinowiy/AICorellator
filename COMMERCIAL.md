## Contribution-Based Pricing

We offer discounts in exchange for contributions to the tool's
development. Contributions are evaluated case-by-case; the discount
reflects the value we perceive. Discounts for different tiers may
overlap; combined discounts never exceed 100%.

### Tier 1 — Significant contribution (80–100% off)

- **Detectors for internal frameworks** that can be included in the
  open core, or as a separately licensed plugin.
- **Public case studies** describing chains found in your projects and
  how AICorellator helped.
- **Funding development** of a specific feature, in exchange for free
  use.
- **Discovered vulnerabilities or shortcomings** in the tool, reported
  responsibly and with enough detail to reproduce and fix.
- **Legal or compliance expertise** — help with licensing, contracts,
  or policy.
- **Access to test environments** that let us validate detection on
  real-world code.

### Tier 2 — Medium contribution (30–70% off)

- **Regular, useful feedback** — at least once a year, structured and
  reproducible.
- **Testing on real projects** — running the tool on your repositories
  and reporting false positives, false negatives, and coverage gaps.
- **Promotion of the tool** — talks, blog posts, internal adoption
  write-ups, recommendations to peers.

### Tier 3 — Basic contribution (20–50% off)

- **Mutual NDA** protecting both parties' confidential information.
- **Prepayment** for one, two, or three years.

### Used-by (additive, not a tier)

**By default, we do not disclose the identity of our licensees.** Your
organization is not mentioned publicly unless you explicitly grant
permission.

Permission to reference your organization publicly as a user of
AICorellator is evaluated separately and added on top of any tier's
discount. You may withdraw the permission at any time; the discount for
the current paid period remains.

### By negotiation (up to 100% off)

For contributions that do not fit the tiers, or that combine several
tiers in an unusual way.

**We value security over revenue.** If your contribution materially
improves the tool, the license may be free.
